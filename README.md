> He finally forgave her. She'd forgotten.

## The story

Two beats, one reversal: years of resentment resolved in "finally", then undone in a single word — she has forgotten. A whole fraught history sits behind six words, and the reader fills in who she is, what she did, and whether she forgot the wrong or forgot him entirely.

## How to run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Deterministic, offline, stdlib-only. Writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`; running it twice writes byte-identical output. The program asserts the gate itself before writing: exactly six words, 1–60 characters.
